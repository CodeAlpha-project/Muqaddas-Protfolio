Abstract

A biotechnology graduate whose curiosity runs from the bench to the sequence file — equally at home running a gel as reading one.

Muqaddas holds a Bachelor of Science in Biotechnology from the Virtual University of Pakistan and has spent the past two years moving between diagnostic laboratories and hospital settings across Punjab and Islamabad, building fluency in molecular biology techniques, clinical diagnostics, and laboratory quality control. Her research interests center on CRISPR-Cas9 genome editing, molecular biotechnology and genetics, and bioinformatics — an interest reflected in a co-authored review on nanotechnology's role in molecular biology and biomedical engineering. She communicates in English, Urdu, and Punjabi.
